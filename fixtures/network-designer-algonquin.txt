BACKGROUND

The Algonquin College of Applied Arts and Technology (AC) is a publicly funded English-
language college and member of Polytechnics Canada located in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.
The College serves the National Capital Region and the outlying areas of Eastern Ontario,
Western Quebec, and Upstate New York. The College has three campuses; a primary
campus located in Ottawa, Ontario, and secondary campuses located in Perth, Ontario,
and Pembroke, Ontario.

The (AC) Information Technology Services (ITS) department provides support services for
approximately 65,000 which includes students, faculty, administrative staff, and distance
learning.

DESCRIPTION OF WORK/ DELIVERABLES

The Network Designer will support the Algonquin College Information Technology Services
(ITS) department by coordinating the design, planning, implementation, testing, commissioning,
and acceptance of structured cabling and associated physical technology infrastructure. The
consultant will report to the Manager, Network and Voice Operations and will serve as the
primary ITS coordination point for cabling activities associated with new construction,
renovations, retrofits, office changes, technology deployments, and other projects affecting
College spaces or infrastructure.

The consultant will work closely with Facilities, architects, engineers, consultants, contractors,
cabling vendors, and College teams including Network, Audio Visual (AV), Endpoint, Security,
and emergency communications. The role will ensure that technology infrastructure
requirements are identified early, incorporated into project designs, implemented correctly,
tested, documented, and accepted in accordance with College standards.

The Network Designer will be responsible for delivering the following:

  1. Coordinate ITS structured cabling requirements for new construction, renovations,
     retrofits, office changes, technology deployments, and other infrastructure projects.
  2. Review architectural, electrical, communications, and construction drawings to confirm
     that ITS requirements are identified and incorporated into project designs.
  3. Coordinate the design and placement of network data outlets, wireless access points,
     emergency phones, emergency broadcast speakers, AV infrastructure, endpoint
     requirements, and Security-related cabling.
  4. Ensure that cable pathways, conduits, cable trays, J-hooks, telecommunications rooms,
     equipment locations, and associated physical infrastructure are appropriately planned
     and designed.
  5. Ensure that project designs and installations comply with the College's ITS
     Communications Infrastructure Specifications, Standards and Practices.
  6. Coordinate structured cabling activities from initial requirements and design through
     procurement, implementation, testing, commissioning, deficiency resolution, and final
     acceptance.
  7. Coordinate with Facilities, consultants, contractors, vendors, Network, AV, Endpoint,
     Security, emergency communications, and other College stakeholders.
  8. Participate in Facilities and construction project meetings to identify ITS impacts,
     dependencies, risks, site-readiness conditions, and infrastructure requirements early in
     the project lifecycle.

 17. Perform basic physical network activities where appropriate, including Ethernet patching,
     installation of patch cables, and connectivity verification.

     documentation, warranties, deficiencies, and supporting information are complete.
 19. Support procurement activities related to structured cabling, fibre infrastructure,

REQUIREMENTS

The ideal candidate must possess the following qualifications:

 1. Post-secondary diploma or degree in telecommunications, information technology,
    electronics, engineering technology, construction technology, or a related field, or an
    equivalent combination of education and relevant experience.
 2. A minimum of five years of progressively responsible experience coordinating structured
    cabling, telecommunications, network infrastructure, or technology components of
    construction and renovation projects in a complex organization.
 3. Demonstrated experience reviewing architectural, electrical,
    communications, and construction drawings and translating technology
    requirements into coordinated designs and implementation activities.
